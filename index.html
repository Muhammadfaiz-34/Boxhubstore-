How do I get a price for my custom boxes?

Share the box style, dimensions, quantity, printing and finishing details using the project form. Pricing is tailored to your specifications; the collection does not display fixed prices.

Can I use my own logo and artwork?

Yes—include your artwork requirements in your inquiry. We’ll discuss the appropriate file format, placement and print specifications for your chosen box.

What if I’m unsure which box to choose?

Tell us about the product, its size and weight, and how you plan to use the packaging. This helps us discuss a suitable structure, material and insert.

Can I request a sample?

Include a sample request with your project details. Sample availability, cost and timing will be confirmed for your chosen specification.

What are the minimum quantities and lead times?

Minimum quantities and schedules depend on the box, material and finish. We’ll confirm the relevant quantities, production timing and delivery arrangements in your quote.
